AFC-AUP-01 • Responsible Work

Acceptable Use Policy

These rules explain what employees may and may not do when using company technology.

Document: AFC-AUP-01 Owner: IT Manager and HR Proposed Version 1.0 Review: Every 6 Months

Responsible Use of Company Systems

✓ Activities Employees May Do

  • Use company computers for authorised sales, stock, shipping and administrative duties.
  • Use Microsoft 365 and company email for work communication.
  • Use approved software and company file storage.
  • Connect approved work devices to employee Wi-Fi.
  • Report phishing messages and faulty equipment.
  • Use limited personal browsing during breaks if permitted by the company.

✕ Activities Employees Must Avoid

  • Sharing usernames, passwords or access cards.
  • Downloading unapproved programs or disabling security tools.
  • Using company devices for harassment, illegal material or pirated content.
  • Sending customer or payroll records to personal storage or public AI tools without approval.
  • Connecting personal routers or unauthorised hotspots.
  • Making large personal downloads that interfere with branch work.

Additional Workplace Rules

Email

Check unusual attachments, links and requests for changed banking details. Confirm sensitive changes using a trusted phone number.

Printers

Check the correct printer and collect confidential pages immediately. Dispose of sensitive documents securely.

Public Product Information

Only authorised marketing staff may approve product updates. Another authorised person should check details before publication.

Employee Responsibility: Employees should acknowledge the approved policy during induction and ask the IT department when they are uncertain about acceptable use.
