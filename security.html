AFC-SEC-01 • Information Protection

IT Security Policy

Guidelines to keep customer, payroll and company information safe in every African Foods branch.

Document: AFC-SEC-01 Owner: IT Manager Proposed Version 1.0 Review: Every 6 Months

Security Rules for Employees

1. Accounts and Passwords

  • Every employee must have an individual account.
  • Use a unique passphrase of at least 14 characters.
  • Enable multifactor authentication for approved email, remote access and administrator accounts.
  • Never share passwords or approve unexpected sign-in requests.

2. Access to Business Information

  • Managers must approve access based on work duties.
  • Payroll data must be restricted to authorised HR and finance staff.
  • HR must tell IT when an employee joins, changes roles or leaves.
  • The IT Manager should review access every quarter.

3. Devices and Networks

  • Keep approved security software and updates active.
  • Lock computers when leaving a desk and encrypt company laptops.
  • Keep guest Wi-Fi separate from employee and server networks.
  • Do not add personal routers or move network equipment without IT approval.

4. Data Storage and Backups

  • Store company files only in approved work locations.
  • Never move payroll or customer files to personal email or unapproved USB devices.
  • Only authorised administrators may manage backups.
  • Report a lost laptop or suspicious access immediately.

What to Do if Something Suspicious Happens

  1. Stop using the affected computer and disconnect its network connection if a compromise is suspected.
  2. Phone the help desk or your branch contact promptly.
  3. Explain what you noticed and when it started.
  4. Do not delete evidence, wipe files or reconnect until IT allows it.
  5. The IT Manager must involve the designated Information Officer and Legal Officer if personal information could be exposed.
