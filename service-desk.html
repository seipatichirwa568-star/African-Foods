IT Help Desk Procedure

Employees should know how to report a problem and what happens after a request is received.

Document: AFC-SD-01 Owner: IT Support Technician Proposed Version 1.0 Review: Every 6 Months

How to Report an IT Problem

  1. Contact the approved IT support channel or your branch contact. Phone immediately for a security incident or major outage.
  2. Provide your name, branch, asset tag and the application or device affected.
  3. Explain when the problem started, the exact error and how it affects your work.
  4. IT creates a ticket and gives the issue an owner and priority.
  5. The technician investigates, records updates and escalates when required.
  6. Before closing the ticket, the employee repeats the failed task to confirm that the problem is resolved.
Important: Never include passwords, full payroll reports or unnecessary customer personal information in an IT support ticket.

Proposed Support Priorities

These are proposed support targets. They require company approval and an agreed support arrangement.

Priority Example Response Time Target
P1 — Critical All branches cannot access central business systems. 15 minutes during agreed support coverage. Workaround or restoration within 4 hours for ordinary outages.
P2 — High A whole branch cannot complete time-critical work. 1 support hour 8 support hours
P3 — Normal One employee has a problem but can still use a workaround. 4 support hours 2 working days
P4 — Request Approved software, access or general IT advice. 1 working day Agree on a completion date within 5 working days.

Escalation and Follow-Up

First-Line Support

The IT Support Technician checks the scope of the problem, power, connectivity and recent changes. Simple issues may be resolved at this stage.

All steps taken should be recorded in the support ticket.

Manager and Specialist Support

Security and P1 incidents are escalated to the IT Manager immediately. Difficult network, server or software problems may be referred to the contracted IT specialist.

The IT department should continue providing progress updates until the issue is resolved.

Example: If the shipping staff in Cape Town cannot print labels before dispatch, the help desk records the business impact, checks the printer and network, and asks the shipping staff to test a label before closing the ticket.
