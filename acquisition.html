AFC-ACQ-01 • Controlled Purchasing

IT Acquisition and Approval

Buying the right equipment involves more than looking at the cheapest price. Requests must be justified, checked and approved.

Document: AFC-ACQ-01 Owner: IT Manager and Accountant Proposed Version 1.0 Review: Every 6 Months

Proposed Purchasing Procedure

Step 1 — Identify the Need. The employee or branch lead explains what is needed, the quantity, branch, expected benefit and estimated price.
Step 2 — Check Existing Resources. The branch lead confirms the business need and checks whether existing equipment can be reused.
Step 3 — Technical Review. The IT Manager checks compatibility, security, warranty, support life and software licensing.
Step 4 — Confirm the Budget. The Accountant checks available funds and all once-off and recurring costs.
Step 5 — Obtain Quotations and Approvals. The correct approvers review supplier options before a purchase order is issued.
Step 6 — Receive and Record Assets. The technician checks the delivery, records serial numbers and tags equipment before issue.
Step 7 — Pay and Close. The Accountant matches the purchase order, delivery record and invoice before payment.

Proposed Approval Limits

These thresholds are recommendations for the case study, not confirmed existing African Foods rules.

Total Cost Quotations Approval
Up to R5 000 At least 1 IT Manager checks technical suitability; Accountant confirms budget.
R5 001 – R25 000 At least 2 The above checks, plus Cape Town Operations Officer approval.
Above R25 000 At least 3 The above checks, plus senior management approval.
Example: A Polokwane employee needs a R12 000 laptop. Our group recommends two quotations, technical checking by IT, budget checking by the Accountant and approval by the Cape Town Operations Officer. The requester must not approve their own purchase.
